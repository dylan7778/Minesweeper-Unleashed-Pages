Common questions

How do I flag and reveal tiles?

Tap a covered tile to safely start a round. After the opening, the default controls are tap to flag and hold to reveal. Use the quick control switch below the board or open Settings to choose your preferred controls and hold duration. The tutorial walks you through the basics.

How does No Guess work?

No Guess generates a board that can be solved using clues alone from your chosen opening. Read the numbers and use neighboring revealed tiles to work out where the mines must be.

Why haven’t my coins appeared in my purse?

In Arcade play, coins collected during a round are banked when you win. The round’s result explains the payout and applicable bonuses. Pro and Classic use their own presentation and do not award Arcade coins.

Where is my saved game?

The current release stores progress on the device where you play. Return to the main menu and choose Resume game when a saved round is available. Game Center and iCloud progress sync are not available in this release. Deleting the app can remove its saved data.

How do I change the music or sound?

Use the speaker control to mute or resume music. Settings includes separate gameplay-music and sound-effects volume controls, music playback options, and an endgame sound setting. Classic and Pro have silent gameplay.

How do I zoom the board?

Pinch to zoom, or use the magnifying-glass control to step through the available zoom levels. When zoomed in, drag to move around the field. Settings lets you choose whether the game remembers a zoom level for each board size.

How can I report a problem with a friend challenge?

Email support with a description of the issue and, if you are comfortable sharing it, the challenge link. Challenge links contain the board information needed to play the same field and may include a result for comparison. Both players need the app installed.
