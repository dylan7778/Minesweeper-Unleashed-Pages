Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Applies to the current 1.0 release

The game does not require an account, contain advertising or third-party analytics, or track you across apps and websites.

Game progress is stored on your device. The current release does not send your gameplay records to a developer-operated server. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send.

Information stored on your device

The game saves information needed to run and remember your experience. This includes saved rounds, local scores and score initials you enter, game history, settings, coins, unlocked items, achievements or milestones recorded locally, and personal pace records. The developer does not automatically receive these local records.

Motion effects

The title screen can use device-motion information to move its artwork as you tilt your device. This information is processed on the device for the visual effect; the game does not retain it or send it to the developer.

Optional sharing

If you choose to share a result or friend challenge, the game passes the selected game information, such as board settings, a board link, and your result, to Apple’s share sheet. You choose the recipient and sharing service. The service you select handles that shared content under its own policies. The game does not upload it to a developer-operated sharing server.

Game Center and iCloud

Game Center features and the game’s own iCloud progress-sync feature are disabled in this release. The game does not submit scores to Game Center or use its progress-sync feature to upload your saves. Your device’s system backups are separate and may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Support correspondence

If you email eresourcesfl@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other details you provide. We use that information to respond to your request and troubleshoot issues. Support correspondence may be retained to resolve your request and maintain a record of the conversation. You may contact the same address to request deletion of support information, subject to any applicable retention requirements.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Advertising, analytics, and sale of information

The game does not include advertising networks, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and local data

You can choose whether to use optional sharing and whether to contact support. You can remove the game’s local data from your device by deleting the app; offloading the app is different and may retain its data. Device backups and restored copies are managed through your Apple settings. There is no developer-operated player account to delete.

Children’s privacy

The game does not ask children or other players to provide personal information to play. If information about a child is sent to support and you would like it removed, contact us at the address below.

Apple services and this website

Apple may process App Store, purchase, backup, or diagnostic information according to the services you use and your device settings. See Apple’s privacy policy for details.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not include analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or cookies set by us. GitHub may process technical request information when you visit under the GitHub privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated and its effective date revised.

Contact
